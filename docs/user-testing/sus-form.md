## Facilitator key (do not hand to participants)

Items are Brooke's original ten (1996), with "system" replaced by "kiosk". Odd-numbered items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9) are positively worded; even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10) are negatively worded.

**Scoring** (as in Appendix G), where `x_i` is the raw 1–5 response to item `i`:

1. Item contribution: `c_i = x_i - 1` for odd items, `c_i = 5 - x_i` for even items. Each `c_i` is in 0–4.
2. SUS score: `S = 2.5 × Σ c_i` (i = 1..10), in 0–100.

A blank item invalidates that form (the score assumes all ten). Don't average it in, and note it in the log.
